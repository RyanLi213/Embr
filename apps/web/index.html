How Embr works

A few taps a day turn into a picture of how your RA moves, so you can plan your week with a little more warmth and a little less guesswork.

  1. 1

    Track your daily health

    Daily check-ins capture your pain, stiffness, fatigue, joint activity, sleep, stress, medications, and other factors that may influence how you're feeling.

  2. 2

    Bring everything together

    Embr combines your check-ins with available health measurements, lifestyle information, and local weather conditions like humidity, wind speed, and atmospheric pressure.

  3. 3

    Meet your personal baseline

    During your first two weeks, Embr begins learning what is normal for you. As you continue checking in and recording flares, it builds a more personalized picture of your RA.

  4. 4

    Get your Flarecast forecast

    Flarecast analyzes your personal patterns to estimate your flare risk over the coming days, displaying your forecast in an easy-to-understand calendar.

Our technology

Powered by Flarecast

Flarecast is Embr's personalized flare prediction engine. It brings together your symptoms, lifestyle patterns, health measurements, and environmental conditions to identify changes that may signal an increased likelihood of a flare.

The more information you log over time, the more personal your insights become.

What is Flarecast?

Private and accessible

Your health. Your data. Your control.

Your information stays on your device. No accounts, no advertising trackers, and no selling your personal health information. Embr is designed to make tracking your RA simple, private, and accessible.

Embr is a wellness and self-tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose and never tells you to change your treatment. If your symptoms are severe or not improving, please contact your rheumatologist.

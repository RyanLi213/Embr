The technology behind Embr

What is Flarecast?

Flarecast is Embr's personalized prediction engine. Instead of comparing you with anyone else, it learns what a usual day looks like for you, then looks for the small changes that tend to come before your flares.

It learns your normal

During your first 14 days, Flarecast works out your personal baseline for each symptom: your typical level and how much it usually varies from day to day. Until then, it shows "Learning your patterns" instead of a forecast.

It watches for change

Each day it compares your last week with your baseline. A higher-than-usual average and a steady climb in symptoms are the strongest early signals. More painful or swollen joints, longer morning stiffness, poor sleep, stress, illness, missed doses and falling air pressure each add a little more.

It adapts to you

Once you've recorded a couple of flares, Flarecast looks at what was happening in the week before each one and gives more weight to the signals that matter most for you. No two forecasts are tuned the same way.

It shows its reasons

For today and the next three days you see a 0 to 100 score, a Low, Moderate or High level, the main reasons in plain language, and how confident it is. Confidence grows as you check in more.

Flarecast shows what your patterns suggest, never a promise. It runs on your device, it isn't a medical device, it doesn't diagnose, and it never tells you to change your treatment.

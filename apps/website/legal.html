Privacy and terms

Your data, your choices.

A plain-language summary of how Embr is designed to handle your information. A full privacy policy and terms of use will be published here before launch.

Privacy

  • Everything you log is stored on your own device, inside the app.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • There are no ads, analytics or third-party trackers.
  • Your personal health information is never sold.
  • Your location is only used to look up local weather, and only if you allow it. You can type a city instead.

Your privacy choices

Because Embr doesn't use advertising or tracking, there is nothing to opt out of. The choices are all yours, inside the app:

  • Turn location for weather on or off, or enter a city by hand.
  • Turn daily check-in reminders on or off.
  • Export a report for your rheumatologist, or your raw data, whenever you like.
  • Delete all of your data at any time.

Terms

Embr's full terms of use will be published here before launch.

In short: Embr is a wellness and self-tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, and it never tells you to start, stop or change any treatment. Forecasts show what your patterns suggest, never a promise. If your symptoms are severe or not improving, please contact your rheumatologist. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
